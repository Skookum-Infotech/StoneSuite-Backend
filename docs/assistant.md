# StoneSuite Assistant

The StoneSuite Assistant is a chat-style helper built into StoneSuite. It answers
two kinds of questions: how to do something in the app, and questions about your
own CRM records (leads, prospects, customers) — including counts, like how many
customers you have. It only answers from what it can actually retrieve, and it
only sees records your role is allowed to see. If it can't find a good answer,
it says so plainly instead of guessing.

## Opening the assistant

Click the Help icon in the top header (it looks like a question mark) and choose
StoneSuite Assistant from the menu. This opens a floating chat panel you can use
from anywhere in the app without leaving your current page. The Help menu also
has a Support option for filing a bug or feature request, which is a separate
tool from the assistant — see the Support/feedback documentation for that.

The assistant is available to signed-in staff users. It is not available to
customer-portal sessions.

## Asking how to use the app

Ask the assistant plain questions about how StoneSuite works, the same way
you'd ask a coworker — "how do I create a quote," "how do I add a user," "what
does the Credit Hold status mean," "how do I invite a customer to the portal."
It answers from StoneSuite's help documentation, so it can walk you through
menu paths, buttons, and statuses by name.

## Asking about your CRM records

Ask about a specific lead, prospect, or customer — "what's the status of the
Acme deal," "who owns the Initech prospect," "summarize the notes on this
lead." The assistant searches your tenant's own records and answers only from
what it finds and what your role lets you see. It can also answer simple count
questions about your CRM records, like "how many customers do we have" or "how
many prospects are there."

## What it can't do

The assistant is best at one clear question at a time about a specific record,
a small set of similar records, or an unfiltered count. It is not a general
reporting tool: questions that mix a count with a filter it doesn't fully
understand (an unusual date range, an outcome, or a status it can't map) may
get an honest "I don't have that information" instead of a number, even though
the records exist. It also can't take actions on your behalf — it only answers
questions, it doesn't create, edit, or delete records.

## Getting a good answer

- Ask one question at a time rather than combining several into one message.
- Be specific: use the record's name or number when you can, rather than a
  vague reference like "that deal."
- For how-to questions, name the screen or feature you're asking about.
- If a previous answer in the conversation is relevant, you can ask a
  follow-up ("what about last month?") and the assistant will use that
  context.

## Follow-up questions and conversation history

The assistant remembers the earlier turns of your current conversation, so
you can ask a follow-up without repeating yourself — for example, asking
about a record and then following up with "what about last month?" and
having it understood in context. Use New Chat in the assistant panel to
start a fresh conversation, or the history icon to reopen one of your
recent conversations. Your conversations are private to you — only you can
see them, whatever CRM scope your role otherwise holds.

## If it says it doesn't have the information

This means it couldn't find a good match for your question — not that the
answer doesn't exist. Try rephrasing with different words, being more
specific about the record name or feature, or splitting a multi-part question
into separate questions. You can also check the relevant page in the app
directly, or ask the assistant a how-to question about where to find it.
