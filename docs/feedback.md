# Reporting a Bug or Requesting a Feature

StoneSuite has a built-in way to send feedback straight to the team building
it, without leaving the app.

## Opening Support

Click the question-mark Help icon in the header and choose Support, or use
the My Tickets entry in the sidebar. This opens the Support page with two
tabs: My Tickets and New Ticket. Support is separate from the StoneSuite
Assistant — the assistant answers questions, while Support is where you
report a problem or ask for something new directly to the team.

## Filing a new ticket

On the New Ticket tab, choose a category — Bug Report, Feature Request, UX /
UI Improvement, Performance Issue, or General Feedback — and, if relevant,
where it happened (Dashboard, CRM, Sales, Purchases, Inventory, Finance,
Configuration, Account / Subscription, or Other). Describe what you like,
what went wrong, or what you'd like to see, and optionally attach a
screenshot or file. Submit the ticket when you're done.

## Tracking your tickets

The My Tickets tab lists every ticket you've filed and lets you follow up on
one that's already open. This is also where the Help menu's Support option
takes you when you have an unread reply waiting — the Help menu and the
sidebar's My Tickets entry both show a small badge when you have a reply you
haven't read yet, so you always know when to check back.

## Ticket statuses

A ticket moves through New, In Progress, Done, or Cancelled as the team
works on it. New means it hasn't been picked up yet; In Progress means
someone is actively working on it; Done means it's resolved; Cancelled means
it won't be worked on. Each status change is added to the ticket's own
timeline, along with any replies, so you can see the full history of what
happened on it.

## Replying to a ticket

Open a ticket from My Tickets to see its full conversation and reply. Use
the reply box at the bottom to write a message back to the team — useful for
adding more detail, answering a question they asked, or confirming a fix
worked. The conversation stays attached to the ticket for as long as it's
open, so you and the team always have the full back-and-forth in one place.

## Attaching a screenshot

When filing a ticket, the optional Screenshot / File field lets you attach a
picture or file that helps explain the issue — for a bug report, a
screenshot of what you're seeing is often the fastest way to show the team
what went wrong.

## Who can use Support

Support is available to every signed-in user, including customer-portal
sessions — everyone has a way to report an issue or ask for something new,
regardless of their role. Unlike most of StoneSuite, Support isn't gated by
a permission, so you'll never need to ask an administrator for access to
file a ticket.
