# Getting Started

StoneSuite is a CRM and business-management app for stone fabrication
businesses. This page orients you to the layout: signing in, the sidebar,
the Dashboard, search, and where to get help.

## Signing in

Go to your organization's StoneSuite sign-in page and enter your email
address, then your password on the next step. If your organization uses
single sign-on, you may instead be sent to your company's identity provider
to sign in there instead of entering a StoneSuite password directly. If you
forget your password, use the "Forgot password" link on the sign-in page to
reset it by email. A new teammate signs in for the first time by accepting
an email invitation an administrator sent them.

## The sidebar and navigation areas

The left sidebar is how you move around StoneSuite. It's organized into
sections:

- **Workspace** — CRM (Leads, Prospects, Customers), Sales (Estimates,
  Quotes, Sales Orders, Installation / Fabrication, Invoices, Payments,
  Credit Memos, Refunds), Purchases (Vendors, Requisitions, Purchase Orders,
  Item Receipts, Vendor Bills, Vendor Payments, Vendor Credits, Expenses),
  Inventory (Items, Units / Slabs, Bundles, Bin Management, Warehouses,
  Adjust Inventory, Transfer Inventory, Inventory Count), and Finance
  (Journal Entries, Chart of Accounts, Accounting Periods, Default Accounts).
- **Configure** — Configuration, where administrators manage Company Info,
  Workflows, Roles & Access, Users, Customer Portal Users, Record Numbering,
  Dashboard Widgets, Inventory Setup, SAML Setup, Audit Log, and Import Data.
- **Account** — Subscription (visible to the tenant owner).
- **Support** — My Tickets, for filing and following up on bugs or feature
  requests.

You only see the sidebar entries your role has access to — anything you
don't have permission for is hidden rather than shown disabled. Click the
Dashboard link at the top of the sidebar any time to return to your home
screen.

## The Dashboard

The Dashboard is your landing page after signing in. It shows a set of
widgets — things like a pipeline summary, key numbers, recent records, and
snapshots of sales, inventory, and accounting activity — chosen by your
administrator for your role. Click Customize on the Dashboard to show or hide
any of the widgets you've been given access to; you can't add a widget your
role wasn't allocated.

## Global search

Use the search bar in the header (or the full Search page) to look up
records, documents, and people across the app by name or number. Search
results are grouped by type and only include what your role can see — a
result you can't access simply won't appear, the same permission and scope
rules that apply everywhere else in StoneSuite apply to search too. Click
any result to jump straight to that record's detail page.

## The Help menu

Click the question-mark icon in the header to open the Help menu. It has two
options: StoneSuite Assistant, a chat helper for how-to questions and
questions about your CRM records, and Support, for reporting a bug, asking
for a feature, or checking a ticket you've already filed. The Assistant
option is only shown to staff users, not customer-portal sessions, since the
assistant works with tenant data a portal login can't reach.

## Your profile and signing out

Click your name and avatar in the top-right corner to open your profile menu.
From here you can go to Account Settings, view your transactions, switch
between roles you've been assigned (or between workspaces, if you're a
customer-portal user with access to more than one), and sign out with Sign
Out at the bottom of the menu. Your current role and email address are
shown at the top of the menu, so you can always confirm who you're signed
in as.

## What's in StoneSuite

- **CRM** — track leads through prospects to customers. See `crm-concepts.md`.
- **Sales documents** — estimates, quotes, sales orders, invoices, payments,
  credit memos, and refunds. See `sales-documents.md`.
- **Fabrication jobs** — installation and fabrication work tied to a sales
  order. See `fabrication.md`.
- **Purchasing** — vendors, requisitions, purchase orders, item receipts,
  vendor bills, payments, and credits. See `purchasing.md`.
- **Inventory** — items, slabs, bundles, bins, warehouses, adjustments,
  transfers, and counts. See `inventory.md`.
- **Accounting** — chart of accounts, journal entries, accounting periods,
  and expenses. See `accounting.md`.
- **Importing data** — bring records in from a file. See `import.md`.
- **Customer portal** — external logins for your customers. See
  `customer-portal.md`.
- **Administration** — users, roles, workflows, and other setup. See
  `administration.md`.
- **StoneSuite Assistant** — the in-app AI helper. See `assistant.md`.
